Arena Ink

A playable comic poster built around bold choices, live brawler silhouettes, heavy black ink, warm paper, and saturated arena color. It is loud where the player acts and quiet where the player reads.

01 / North star

Fun at first glance

The UI should feel like it belongs to a fast arena brawler before the player reads a word. Shape, contour, and composition carry the mood; live data stays honest and legible.

Hierarchy

One hero action

Deploy, Select, Continue, and match outcomes get the loudest shape and color.

Character

Sticker silhouette

The live 3D brawler gains a black contour, rounded paper keyline, and mechanic-derived motif without flattening the model.

Restraint

Density has a budget

Halftone, bursts, and speed lines frame hero moments; telemetry and tools remain calm.

02 / Color

Poster pigments

Warm paper and near-black ink establish the printed base. Blue navigates, yellow selects, red commits or warns, and purple carries super state. Team colors stay semantically stable.
