02 / HUD rules

Every pixel has a job

Combat UI answers four questions in order: What wins the mode? Am I safe? What can I use? What just happened?

Objective

Top center

Score, win pressure, countdown, and objective alerts share one stable container.

Survival

Body anchored

Points sit inside compact world bars. Allies stay green, enemies stay red, and the player’s equal-height health and ammo rails read as one unit.

Abilities

State, not spectacle

Cooldown, ready, active, and unavailable states use fill, border, label, and key binding.

Feedback

Combat ticker

Incoming values sit left, healing sits right, and outgoing damage stays on its target; rapid values stack upward without shaking the camera.

Previous behaviorImplemented treatmentReason
Several independent high-frequency pulsesOne shared ready/urgent cadence; reduced-motion alternativeLess visual noise and fatigue
Tutorial shown for a fixed opening durationDismiss each prompt after the demonstrated action; remember progressTeaches without covering repeat matches
Victory/defeat then automatic returnPoster with Continue, Rematch, and Change Brawler plus a safe timeoutGives the match a satisfying close
Team information leans on huePair hue with side, icon, label, and bar silhouetteImproves rapid recognition and accessibility
Screen shake hooks can affect attacksKeep combat feedback in VFX, audio, hit stop, and UI responseProtects comfort and aiming clarity

Inspector, not menu

The command center remains deliberately denser than player UI. It shares fonts, semantic state colors, and focus behavior while keeping its own precise editing grammar.
