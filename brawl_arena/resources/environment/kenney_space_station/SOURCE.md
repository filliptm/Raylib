# Kenney Space Station Kit

- Source: https://kenney.nl/assets/space-station-kit
- Version: 1.0
- Created: 2024-04-10
- License: Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0)

This directory keeps the pack's runtime-ready GLB models and the two compatible
512×512 color atlases. The FBX, OBJ, preview images, sample scene, and downloaded ZIP
are intentionally excluded.

The original GLBs resolve their default atlas through `models/Textures/colormap.png`.
`models/Textures/variation-a.png` is the pack's purple-accent material variation and is
selected explicitly by Brawl Arena's environment renderer.
